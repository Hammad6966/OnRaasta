How OnRaasta IoT Works
A compact OBD-II Bluetooth dongle plugs into the vehicle's diagnostic port. It reads engine codes (DTCs), live sensor data (RPM, coolant temp, battery voltage), and transmits in real-time to the OnRaasta app via BLE. The AI engine cross-references codes with our fault database to auto-suggest diagnosis before the mechanic even arrives.
